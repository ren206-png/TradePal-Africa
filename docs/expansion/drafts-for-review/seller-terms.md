# DRAFT for your review: TradePal Africa seller terms

**Status: draft written by Claude for the owner to review. Not legal advice. Not published anywhere. Nothing in the product shows these terms yet.** A lawyer in each country where shops go live must review and adjust this text before any seller is asked to accept it. Square brackets [LIKE THIS] are blanks you or the lawyer must fill. Wording is deliberately plain so a shop owner can read it on a phone.

How this draft matches what the software does today is listed at the end ("What the product already enforces"), so you can check that the promises below are ones the code keeps.

## TradePal Africa Seller Terms

*Version [1.0] - date [DD Month YYYY]. These terms are between **[Renco Enterprises legal name and registration number]** ("TradePal Africa", "we") and you, the person or business that opens an online shop with TradePal Africa ("you", "the seller").*

### 1. What TradePal Africa provides
1.1 We provide software that lets you list your products, take orders from shoppers, keep your stock and your books, and, where you choose, show your shop in the TradePal marketplace.
1.2 **You are the seller. We are not.** The sale is between you and the shopper. We do not buy, hold, inspect, store, ship or deliver goods, and we are not a party to the sale.
1.3 We do not hold your customers' money. [If and when an online payment provider is added: payments are taken by [provider], under the provider's own terms, and are paid to you [how, when]. Until then, shop orders are paid in cash, mobile money or bank transfer directly between you and the shopper, and you record them.]

### 2. Who may open a shop
2.1 You must be 18 or over, or a registered business, and allowed to trade in [COUNTRY].
2.2 Give us true information about yourself and your shop. Tell us if it changes.
2.3 One person's phone number can run a shop through WhatsApp. You are responsible for anyone you let use it (staff, family). You can sign every phone out by sending /disconnect.

### 3. What you may sell
3.1 Only goods you are legally allowed to sell in [COUNTRY], that you own or have the right to sell, and that are as you describe them.
3.2 Not allowed: [stolen or counterfeit goods; weapons; illegal drugs; goods that need a licence you do not hold; adult content; anything the law of COUNTRY forbids; any other category we list at [link]].
3.3 You decide your prices. They must be the prices a shopper actually pays, in the shop's currency.

### 4. Your listings, prices and photos
4.1 Keep your stock count honest. If you cannot fill an order, cancel it quickly and say why.
4.2 **Photos.** Upload only photos you took or have the right to use. A photo must show the product you are selling. Photos are stored by TradePal Africa and shown on your shop page and, if you list there, in the marketplace. Do not upload photos of people without their permission, or anything unlawful or misleading. You can remove a photo at any time.
4.3 You are responsible for the words, prices and photos in your shop. We may hide a listing that breaks these terms or the law (section 11).

### 5. Orders, delivery and hand-over
5.1 When a shopper orders, we hold the stock for them for a limited time ([60 minutes] for online payment, [48 hours] for pay-on-collection). Confirm or cancel promptly.
5.2 **Delivery is your responsibility.** If you use a courier or your own rider, you remain responsible for the goods until the shopper has them. A courier saying "delivered" does not finish the order.
5.3 **The hand-over code.** The shopper has a 6-digit code on their order page. An order is completed only when you enter that code (or, in the rare case there is no code, you record a written reason). Do not ask for or accept the code before the goods are in the shopper's hands.
5.4 If a delivery fails, tell us in the app (/store failed ...): the goods go back into your stock and the order is cancelled.
5.5 TradePal Africa may show a shopper the courier's name, the delivery status, a tracking link and the rider's name and phone number that you give us. Only give details of people who agree to be shown.
5.6 If you book a courier through TradePal Africa, the shopper's name, phone number and delivery address are passed to that courier **only** so it can deliver, together with a list of the items (never prices). You must have the courier's own terms in place and you are responsible for how the courier treats that data.

### 6. Cancellations, refunds and returns
6.1 You follow the cancellation and refund rules shown on your shop and order pages. They are the same rules the app applies.
6.2 When a shopper asks to cancel, return or be refunded, **you decide within 5 days.** If you do not, TradePal Africa may open a support case and contact you; it can ask you to act but does not decide for you, and it does not move your money.
6.3 If a refund is due, you pay it. Where TradePal Africa records a refund, that is a record, not a payment.
6.4 A shopper's right under the law of [COUNTRY] to a refund, repair or replacement is not reduced by these terms.

### 7. Disputes with shoppers
7.1 A shopper may report a problem with an order. You are expected to respond fairly and quickly. A support case names who is responsible (usually you, the seller).
7.2 We may share what you wrote and what the order record shows with the shopper and, for a payment problem, with the payment provider.
7.3 We are not a court. For a dispute we cannot settle, [governing law and courts / ombudsman in COUNTRY] apply.

### 8. Money, fees and records
8.1 [TradePal Africa's fees, if any, for the shop: describe here. Today: none for orders; the TradePal subscription is separate and is on the plans page.]
8.2 The sales, expenses and stock figures in the app are your own records. They are not tax returns or accounts, and not professional advice. You are responsible for your own taxes in [COUNTRY].
8.3 We keep the financial records of your orders (amounts, items, dates) for [N] years. See section 10 for personal data.

### 9. Shopper information
9.1 You will see a shopper's name, phone number, delivery address and note so that you can fulfil the order. Use them **only** for that order and for responding to the shopper about it. Do not sell them, post them, or message the shopper for marketing without their separate agreement.
9.2 **Messages.** TradePal Africa may send a shopper order updates on WhatsApp only if the shopper asked for them and confirmed from their own phone number. These messages are about the order only, never marketing. You must not ask us to send anything else.
9.3 TradePal Africa removes a shopper's name, phone number, address and notes from finished orders **24 months** after the order's last update, and keeps the amounts and the accounting records. Do not keep your own copy longer than you need it.

### 10. Your data
10.1 Our privacy policy at [link] explains how we handle personal data. You can ask us to export or delete your account and data by [contact].
10.2 If you close your shop, the records we must keep by law remain.

### 11. Suspending or closing a shop
11.1 We may hide a listing or suspend a shop if we reasonably believe it breaks these terms or the law, if there are repeated unfulfilled orders, or if there are serious complaints. We will tell you why, and you can reply to explain or appeal (/store appeal ...).
11.2 You can close your shop at any time (/store close). Orders already placed must still be completed or cancelled and refunded properly.

### 12. Our responsibility
12.1 We work to keep the service running but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. [Limitation of liability and warranties: lawyer to complete, consistent with the law of COUNTRY.]
12.2 We are not responsible for what you sell, for a courier's or rider's actions, for a shopper's behaviour, or for a payment provider's service.
12.3 You will cover losses we suffer because you broke these terms or the law. [lawyer to review]

### 13. Changes
13.1 We may change these terms. We will tell you [30] days before a change that matters; continuing to use the shop after that means you accept it.

## What the product already enforces (so the promises above are true)
| Clause | Where it is kept |
|---|---|
| 3.3 / 4.1 prices and stock | listing needs a retail price; stock holds reserve real units; an order cannot oversell (tested, including on a real Postgres under parallel orders) |
| 4.2 photos | stored by us, never loaded from a seller's link; JPEG/PNG/WebP only, 400 KB, 6 per product; removing a photo deletes its bytes |
| 5.1 holds | 60 minutes online / 48 hours pay-on-collection |
| 5.2-5.3 delivery and the code | a courier's "delivered" never completes an order; completion needs the shopper's code or a written reason |
| 5.4 failed delivery | `/store failed` returns stock and cancels |
| 6.2 five days | requests unanswered for 5 days open a support case automatically |
| 9.2 messages | double opt-in from the shopper's own number; order updates only; STOP honoured platform-wide |
| 9.3 retention | 24-month removal of shopper personal fields, daily (`SHOPPER_DATA_RETENTION_MONTHS`) |
| 11.1 suspension | admin can suspend a shop; listings and photos disappear from the public pages at once |
